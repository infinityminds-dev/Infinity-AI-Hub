# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Infinity AI Hub to be a welcoming and respectful project for everyone.

By participating in this project, contributors and community members are expected to treat each other with respect and help maintain a positive environment.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- 🤝 Being respectful and considerate toward others.
- 🗣️ Communicating clearly and constructively.
- 💡 Providing useful feedback and suggestions.
- 🧑‍💻 Focusing on improving the project rather than attacking people.
- 🔐 Respecting the privacy and personal information of others.
- 📚 Being open to learning and different technical perspectives.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful comments.
- Deliberate intimidation or bullying.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Malicious behavior toward contributors or the project.
- Any other conduct that would make the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Contribution Discussions

Technical disagreements are normal.

When disagreements happen, discuss the code, ideas, or implementation—not the person.

Try to explain your reasoning and remain respectful even when opinions differ.

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer through the contact or reporting method provided by the repository.

Please do not publicly expose someone's private information when reporting an issue.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when this Code of Conduct is violated.

Actions may include removing inappropriate content, restricting participation, or taking other reasonable steps to protect the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within the Infinity AI Hub project, including repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project-related community spaces.

## Final Note

Infinity AI Hub is an open-source project built for learning, experimentation, and collaboration.

Let's keep the community respectful, constructive, and focused on building great software together. 🧠🤝
